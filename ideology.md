## Technical Philosophy

### 1. Minimalist Design & Performance
Fast-loading, clean interfaces with strategic whitespace. Less visual clutter means more mental room for imagination. Focus on single-page experiences, lazy loading, and optimized assets. The technical execution should feel effortless.

### 2. Interactive Storytelling
Subtle animations, scroll-triggered reveals, and micro-interactions that suggest rather than dictate. Let users discover and interpret, making them active participants rather than passive consumers. Create memorable moments through interaction.

### 3. Conceptual Navigation
Non-traditional layouts that intrigue and invite exploration. Progressive disclosure where information unfolds based on curiosity rather than overwhelming upfront. The code should be invisible—clean architecture, smooth performance, accessibility—so the experience dominates, not the technology itself."
